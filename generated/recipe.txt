Til ladoo (Sesame Seed Balls) are a popular Indian sweet, especially during festivals like Makar Sankranti and Lohri. Here's how to make them:

**Ingredients:**

* 1 cup white sesame seeds (til)
* 1/2 cup jaggery (gur), roughly chopped or grated (adjust to your sweetness preference)
* 2 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter)
* Optional: 1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder (elaichi)

**Instructions:**

1. **Roast the sesame seeds:**  In a dry pan over medium heat, roast the sesame seeds, stirring constantly, until they turn light golden brown and fragrant.  Be careful not to burn them. This usually takes about 5-7 minutes.  Immediately transfer the roasted sesame seeds to a plate or tray to cool.
2. **Melt the jaggery:** In the same pan (no need to wash), add the ghee.  Once the ghee melts, add the chopped or grated jaggery. Stir continuously over low heat until the jaggery melts completely and forms a smooth syrup.  To check for consistency, drop a small amount of the syrup into a bowl of cold water. It should form a soft ball that doesn't stick to your fingers.
3. **Combine and mix:** Add the roasted sesame seeds (make sure they are completely cooled) and optional cardamom powder to the melted jaggery. Mix well until the sesame seeds are evenly coated with the jaggery syrup.
4. **Shape into ladoos:** While the mixture is still warm, grease your palms with a little ghee. Take small portions of the mixture and quickly shape them into round ladoos.  The mixture will start to harden as it cools, so work quickly.
5. **Cool and serve:** Place the prepared ladoos on a plate or tray lined with parchment paper and let them cool completely.  Once cooled, the ladoos will firm up. Store them in an airtight container at room temperature for up to a week.


**Tips for perfect Til Ladoo:**

* **Roasting the sesame seeds:** Roasting the sesame seeds is crucial for flavor.  Don't skip this step, and be careful not to over-roast or they'll become bitter.
* **Jaggery consistency:** Getting the right jaggery consistency is key to binding the ladoos.  The soft-ball test is the best way to ensure the syrup is ready.
* **Working quickly:** The mixture hardens quickly as it cools, so work fast when shaping the ladoos.  If the mixture becomes too hard, you can gently heat it again for a few seconds to soften it.
* **Variations:**  You can add other ingredients like chopped nuts (peanuts, cashews), desiccated coconut, or roasted chana dal (split chickpeas) for added flavor and texture.

Enjoy your homemade Til Ladoo!
